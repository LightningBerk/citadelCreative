Asa is a multimedia specialist and web developer with years of hands-on experience across 3D modeling, photography, videography, and e-commerce. At Willington Design & Print, he creates 3D renders, manages online storefronts, and leads social media content strategy.

A former 3D artist at Legion Studios—where his game assets hit 40K+ downloads—and a Vermont State Champion in Game Design, Asa now leverages AI-driven creative workflows to accelerate execution without sacrificing craftsmanship. He architects every Citadel project from strategy to ship.
